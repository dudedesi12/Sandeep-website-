Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 February 2026

1. Introduction

LoanMate Finance Pty Ltd ("LoanMate Finance", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. We are bound by the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use, and disclose your personal information, including credit-related information, and how you can access or correct information we hold about you.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and hold the following types of personal information:

2.1 Identity & Contact Information

  • Full name, date of birth, and gender
  • Residential and postal address
  • Phone number(s) and email address
  • Driver's licence, passport, or other identification documents

2.2 Financial Information

  • Employment details and income information
  • Assets, liabilities, and financial commitments
  • Bank statements and transaction histories
  • Tax returns and business financial statements
  • Existing loan and credit card details

2.3 Credit Information

  • Credit report information from credit reporting bodies
  • Credit scores and credit history
  • Details of credit applications and outcomes
  • Repayment history

2.4 Website & Technical Information

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Referring website or search terms used
  • Device information and operating system
  • Information from enquiry forms submitted on our website

3. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information through:

  • Direct interaction: When you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or in person, or when you complete our online enquiry form
  • Loan applications: During the loan application and assessment process
  • Third parties: From credit reporting bodies, lenders, employers, accountants, solicitors, and real estate agents with your consent
  • Automated collection: Through cookies and analytics when you visit our website
  • Public sources: From publicly available sources where permitted by law

Where practicable, we will collect information directly from you. We will always let you know when information is collected from third parties.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To assess your borrowing needs and eligibility for finance products
  • To compare and recommend suitable loan products from our panel of lenders
  • To prepare and submit loan applications on your behalf
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry, application, or existing loan
  • To comply with our legal obligations under credit legislation
  • To improve our services and website experience
  • To send you relevant updates or offers (only with your consent)
  • To respond to complaints and resolve disputes

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We may disclose your personal information to:

  • Lenders and financial institutions on our panel for the purpose of loan applications
  • Credit reporting bodies to obtain credit reports and verify identity
  • Our aggregator as part of compliance and lodgement processes
  • Professional advisors such as solicitors, accountants, and valuers, as necessary for your loan
  • Regulatory bodies such as ASIC, AFCA, or other bodies as required by law
  • Service providers who assist with website hosting, data storage, and email services (bound by confidentiality agreements)

We will never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

We take the protection of your personal information seriously. Our security measures include:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted through our website is protected with SSL/TLS encryption
  • Access controls: Personal information is accessible only to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis
  • Secure storage: Electronic records are stored in secure, access-controlled cloud systems (Firebase/Google Cloud) with enterprise-grade security
  • Physical security: Paper documents are stored in locked facilities and securely destroyed when no longer required
  • Spam protection: Our website forms include anti-spam measures and rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Regular review: We regularly review our security practices and update them as required
  • Breach protocol: In the event of a data breach, we follow the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme and will notify affected individuals and the OAIC as required

7. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

7.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (e.g., remembering your cookie preference). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website using Google Analytics. These collect anonymised, aggregate data only.

7.2 Managing Cookies

When you first visit our site, a cookie consent banner will appear allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

7.3 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymised information about website traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies to track page views, session duration, and user behaviour. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

8. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of any inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date information
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw your consent for us to use your information for marketing communications at any time
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint if you believe your privacy has been breached

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

9. Credit Reporting

As a mortgage broker, we may need to access your credit report as part of the loan assessment process. We work with the following credit reporting bodies:

  • Equifax — www.equifax.com.au
  • Illion — www.illion.com.au
  • Experian — www.experian.com.au

You may request these bodies not to use your credit reporting information for pre-screening of direct marketing by a credit provider. You may also request access to your credit report directly from these bodies.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Loan-related records are retained for a minimum of 7 years after the last interaction, as required by credit legislation
  • Website enquiry data is retained for 2 years and then securely deleted
  • Marketing consent records are retained until you withdraw consent

When personal information is no longer required, it is securely destroyed or de-identified.

11. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., lender websites, government resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with the revised date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

13. Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles, you may lodge a complaint with us. We will investigate your complaint and respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate your complaint to:

  • Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)
    Phone: 1300 363 992
    Website: www.oaic.gov.au
  • Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)
    Phone: 1800 931 678
    Website: www.afca.org.au
